The rules

  1. If it swam in the sea, yes.
  2. If it swam in a river, yes, once it's been frozen.
  3. If it lived in a shell, yes.
  4. If it's a mammal that swims, yes, if.
  5. If it walked on land, no.
  6. If it flew, probably.
  7. If it's a firm fruit or vegetable, yes.
  8. If it has an engine, a plug or a feeling, no.

The maths: ceviche takes about 240ml of lime juice per 450g of flesh, enough to cover it. A Persian lime gives about 30ml of juice and weighs about 67g. So that's one lime per 56g, rounded up. With no weight given, it's a 150g starter portion, or the whole thing if it comes whole.

Lime juice firms protein. It does not cook it. Buy fish from someone who knows you're eating it raw.
